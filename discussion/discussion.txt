(1) RAG vs. Raw Comparison (topk=3, max_new_tokens=128)
Path: discussion/output/original.txt

In the RAG answer, the LLM is asked to answer the question using the provided relevant information. While in the raw answer, the LLM is asked to answer the question directly without any context.

The RAG answer has answered the question accurately and precisely: “Queen’s University is located in Kingston, Ontario, Canada.” While in the raw answer, it continuously generated a sequence of “question and answer” dialogues, some of which are not relevant to the question.

I think the problem is due to how the LLM is trained. It aims to continue the prompt in a conversational style. However, with the assistance of RAG, it may explicitly tell the LLM the boundary of the conversation and make it focus on the provided information, thus answering the question more accurately.
